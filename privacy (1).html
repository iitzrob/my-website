Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

What I collect

My bots may store Discord IDs, usernames, roles and settings so that features like tickets, applications and moderation work. Some features, such as logging and ticket transcripts, save messages and events from your server.

How it is used

Data is only used to run the features you asked for. It is never sold, shared or leaked to anyone.

Logs and views

Logs and ticket transcripts can only be seen by your server's staff and by me when I need to fix a problem. I do not look at your data without a reason.

Storage and security

Data is kept on my private server and access is limited to me. I take reasonable steps to keep it safe, but no system is perfectly secure.

Website

This website does not ask for personal information. Links to TikTok and Discord open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

You can ask me to delete your data or your server's data at any time, and I will remove it.

Contact

Message me on Discord @iitzrob or TikTok @iitzrob.
